---
title: "Yogurt"
layout: recipe
servings: "15"
ingredients:
  - name: "milk"
    quantity: "3"
    unit: "L"
  - name: "yogurt with live cultures"
    quantity: "150"
    unit: "g"
---

In a large pot, slowly heat milk to 165°F, stirring often to avoid scorching. Hold at that temperature for 10 minutes.

Remove the milk from the heat and cool to 115°F.

Divide the yogurt with live cultures into jars and liquefy by mixing with a small amount of the milk. Pour the remaining milk into the jars and shake well.

Proof at 120°F for 1 hour.

Lower the temperature to 86°F and proof overnight (6-8 hours).

Refrigerate for several hours to fully set.
