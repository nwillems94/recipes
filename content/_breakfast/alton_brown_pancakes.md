Sift together dry ingredients. In a separate bowl, whisk together wet ingredients. Combine the two and mix until just combined. Do not overmix. Let the batter rest for 10 minutes. Heat a griddle or pan over medium heat and lightly grease it. Pour 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake and cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown on the other side.
