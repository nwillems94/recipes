---
title: "Yogurt chili-crisp chicken"
layout: recipe
servings: "4"
ingredients:
  - name: "chicken thighs (preferably boneless & skinless)"
    quantity: "2"
    unit: "lbs"
  - name: "Yogurt"
    quantity: "0.75"
    unit: "cup"
  - name: "Chili crisp"
    quantity: "4"
    unit: "tbsp"
---

Season chicken with salt and pepper.

In a large bowl, whisk together yogurt and chili-crisp, add chicken and toss to coat.

Arrange on baking sheet, top with any remaining yogurt mixture, and bake at 425°F for 25-30 minutes (until thickest part registers 165°F).

Optionally spread cooked rice on baking sheet with chicken juices and bake for 12-15 minutes until juices have been absorbed and rice begins crisping in spots.
