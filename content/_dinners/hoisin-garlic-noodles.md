---

Cook noodles to al dente, drain, and rinse until noodles are cool. 

In a small bowl, combine hoisin sauce, soy sauce, sesame oil, and maple syrup/honey.

In a large, pre-heated skillet over medium-high, fry garlic and white parts of the scallions in vegetable oil until fragrant (about 30 seconds).

Immediately add the sauce and then the noodles, and toss well until the noodles are evenly coated. Leave the noodles to cook, undisturbed, until they start sticking to the pan and the bottom looks crispy (2 to 3 minutes). 

Top with the sesame seeds and the green parts of the scallions.
