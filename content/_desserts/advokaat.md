---
title: "Advokaat"
layout: recipe
servings: "2"
ingredients:
  - name: "vanilla extract"
    quantity: "1"
    unit: "tsp"
  - name: "granulated sugar"
    quantity: "170"
    unit: "g"
  - name: "egg yolks"
    quantity: "8"
  - name: "water"
    quantity: "125"
    unit: "ml"
  - name: "grain alcohol"
    quantity: "110"
    unit: "ml"
---

Heat the water, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract until the sugar is dissolved.

Let cool, then whisk vigorously while adding:
- condensed milk
- then egg yolks, one by one
- then grain alcohol

Portion into jars and let stand in the fridge for at least 8 days.
